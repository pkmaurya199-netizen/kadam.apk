Privacy Policy

Application: Kadam: Walk & Earn (Package: app.kadam.walkearn)
Last Updated & Effective Date: November 3, 2026

🔒 Our Core Commitment: Kadam exists to promote healthy lifestyle habits by rewarding walking. We respect your personal privacy. We NEVER sell, rent, or trade your personal or fitness data to any third-party advertisers or brokers.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Kadam: Walk & Earn mobile application ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our Android mobile application and associated services.

By downloading, installing, or using Kadam, you consent to the data practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the application.

2. Information We Collect

To provide accurate step tracking, gamified rewards, and store features, we collect the following categories of data:

  • Physical Activity & Sensor Data (Core Feature):
    • We request permission for android.permission.ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION and run a Foreground Health Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_HEALTH) to interface with your mobile device's built-in hardware step counter sensor (Sensor.TYPE_STEP_COUNTER).
    • We read your daily step count, estimated walking distance (kilometers), and estimated active calories burned (kcal).
    • Note: We do NOT collect continuous real-time GPS locations or track your geographical routes.
  • Account & Profile Information:
    • When you sign in using Google Sign-In / Firebase Authentication, we collect your display name, email address, and authentication user ID (UID).
    • Optional profile details such as phone number and preferred language (Hindi/English).
  • Rewards, Gamification & Transaction Data:
    • Coins balance earned, daily streak counters, level progression, badges unlocked, challenge participation, and product redemption order history.
  • Technical & Device Information:
    • Device model, operating system version, and anonymous crash diagnostics to ensure app stability and optimal battery performance.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To convert verified physical steps into in-app Kadam coins.
  • To track daily, weekly, and monthly challenges and calculate achievement badges.
  • To manage and deliver store redemption orders.
  • To display your rank on community leaderboards (using your chosen display name).
  • To prevent fraudulent step spoofing and ensure fair reward distribution.
  • To provide customer support and notify you of important app updates.

4. Data Storage and Security

We implement strict administrative, technical, and physical security measures:

  • Daily step counts and local UI states are processed on your device and synchronized securely over encrypted HTTPS (TLS 1.3) protocols.
  • Authentication tokens are securely managed via Google Firebase Authentication.
  • We retain your account data only as long as your account remains active or as required by applicable laws.

5. Third-Party Services

Our application integrates trusted third-party SDKs that operate under their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: Used for core Android framework support and authentication. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Firebase Authentication: Used for secure user sign-in and account management. (Firebase Privacy Policy)

6. Children's Privacy

Kadam is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately, and we will delete the data.

7. Your Rights and Account Deletion

You have full ownership and control over your personal data:

  • Access and Update: You can view and edit your profile details anytime within the Account tab of the app.
  • Revoke Permissions: You can revoke Activity Recognition or notification permissions at any time via your Android device's Settings.
  • Account Deletion: In compliance with Google Play Store policies, you can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time:
    • In-App: Open Kadam > Go to Account tab > Tap Delete Account.
    • Web Request: Visit our dedicated Account Deletion Page to request deletion without opening the app.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
