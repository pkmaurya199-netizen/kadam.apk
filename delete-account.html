Privacy Policy

Request Account & Data Deletion

Official Account Deletion Portal for Kadam: Walk & Earn (app.kadam.walkearn) in compliance with Google Play Store Policies.

⚠️ Warning: Account deletion is permanent!

Once your account is deleted, your coin balance, active streaks, unlocked badges, and order history cannot be recovered.

How Deletion Works

You can delete your Kadam account using either of the two methods below:

Option 1: In-App Instant Deletion (Fastest)

  1. Open the Kadam app on your Android device.
  2. Go to the Account tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the bottom of the page.
  4. Tap the red "Delete account" button and confirm. Your local data and session are cleared immediately.

Option 2: Web Deletion Request (Without App)

If you have uninstalled the app or lost access to your device, please fill out the form below. Our support team will process your request within 7 business days.

✅ Deletion Request Received!

Your account deletion request has been logged. Our data protection team will verify your registered email and permanently erase your account and associated fitness records within 7 business days.

What Data Gets Deleted?

  • Profile & Authentication: Name, Google email, user ID, profile settings.
  • Fitness & Activity History: Lifetime steps, daily step history, distance, calories burned.
  • Rewards & Gamification: Unused coin balances, streak records, level progression, and 84 achievement badges.
  • Store Orders: Delivery address and order logs are permanently anonymized or purged.

Data Retention Policy

We do not retain personal fitness data once account deletion is completed. In rare cases where financial transaction records or fraud investigation requires archival under local taxation and legal compliance, minimal anonymized transaction IDs may be retained for the legally prescribed statutory period.
